About

About This Project

This project leverages machine learning to assist farmers and veterinary professionals in the early detection and diagnosis of common cattle diseases based on reported symptoms.

The application combines powerful classifiers — Random Forest, Naive Bayes, and Decision Tree — to analyze multiple symptoms selected by the user. A consensus voting mechanism ensures the most reliable prediction when two or more models agree.

Additionally, a CNN-based image detection module enables visual analysis of cattle photographs for signs of disease, making the platform a comprehensive diagnostic tool.

This tool can be invaluable in rural and resource-constrained environments where quick access to expert veterinary services may be limited. It empowers users by offering probable diagnoses that can guide further professional examination.
